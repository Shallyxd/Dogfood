How these rankings were made

Method card

Raw scores are converted to per-judge z-scores before ranking, so a harsh judge's 3 and a lenient judge's 3 stop being the same number. Projects with few reviews are pulled toward the global mean (Bayesian shrinkage). Judges with fewer than 2 reviews are excluded from normalisation — their raw scores stay in the CSV export. Flat raters (zero variance) are mean-centred: no signal, no noise. Full guards in JUDGING.md.

Formula: shrunk_score = (n × mean_z + global_mean) / (n + 1)

Measured on fixture data: judge harshness spread (std of per-judge means) before = 0.42 → after = 0.00. Rank movements mostly 0–1, max ±4 places.

Chance of top {{ top_places }}: judges are resampled with replacement, the normaliser is refitted, and we count how often each project finishes in the prize places. A high number is a stable winner; a middling number means the place rests on which judges happened to review it. Deterministic (fixed seed), so the figure is reproducible.
