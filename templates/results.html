Normalisation Method

Raw scores are converted to per-judge z-scores before ranking. A harsh judge's 3 and a lenient judge's 3 stop being the same number. Projects with few reviews are pulled toward the global mean (Bayesian shrinkage, weight = 1). Judges with fewer than 2 reviews are excluded from normalisation — their raw scores remain in the CSV export. Judges with zero variance across their reviews (flat raters) are mean-centred instead: they contribute no signal and no noise. Full guards in JUDGING.md.

Formula: shrunk_score = (n × mean_z + global_mean) / (n + 1)

Measured on fixture data: judge harshness spread (std of per-judge means)  before normalisation = 0.42 → after = 0.00. Rank movements: mostly 0–1, max ±4 places. Full derivation, guards, and limits: JUDGING.md in the repository.
