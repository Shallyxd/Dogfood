How it works

  • Participants submit projects to a track before the deadline — late submissions are refused server-side (HTTP 422).
  • Judges score only their assigned projects on functionality, quality and innovation (2–5). Peer scores are unreachable — enforced in the route, not the UI.
  • Organisers batch-assign judges, tune rubric weights, and publish an immutable ranking snapshot (re-publish is blocked with HTTP 409).
