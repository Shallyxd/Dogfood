How it works

Participants

Submit before the deadline

Create a team, build an invite link, and submit a project to a track. Late entries are refused server-side with HTTP 422 — the deadline is real, not cosmetic.

Judges

Calibrate, review, score

Score only your assigned projects on functionality, quality and innovation. A calibration mirror shows your scale against the pool — and every artefact you open is receipted.

Organisers

Normalise and publish

Batch-assign judges, tune rubric weights, watch progress, then publish an immutable ranking snapshot. Export CSV at every stage.
